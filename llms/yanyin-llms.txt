# Yanyin Technology (衍因科技)

> Shanghai-based biomedical AI company building 衍因智研云 (Yanyin Zhiyun), a digital research-collaboration platform for life-sciences R&D. Its enterprise AI MEGASphere suite includes a Mega Open Platform with an API gateway (API Key and OAuth2 client-credentials auth, rate limiting, request signing, idempotency, versioning), a developer center (online docs, SDKs, sandbox), and webhook callback management with HMAC signing. Backed by Qiming Venture Partners. Holds ISO 9001, ISO 27001, ISO 20000 and China MLPS Level 3.

## Products
- [Mega Open Platform (开放平台)](https://www.yanyin.tech/product/mega-open-platform): API gateway, developer center, data integration center, webhook callbacks
- [Yan Note (电子实验记录)](https://www.yanyin.tech/product/yan-note): electronic lab notebook
- [Yan Molecule (分子生物学工具)](https://www.yanyin.tech/product/yan-molecule): molecular cloning, primer design, sequence editing, biobank
- [Yan LIMS](https://www.yanyin.tech/product/yan-lims): sample, material and inspection management
- [Yan Research](https://www.yanyin.tech/product/yan-research): AI literature interpretation, experiment summary, CRISPR design
- [Yan Reviewer](https://www.yanyin.tech/product/yan-reviewer): AI review and IND application assistance
- [Yan Advisor](https://www.yanyin.tech/product/yan-advisor): AI research advisor
